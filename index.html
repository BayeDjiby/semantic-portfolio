About Me

My Technical & Creative Outlook

I view web development and media production through the same lens: both demand structural precision, intentional pacing, and absolute clarity. In a digital landscape overwhelmed by framework overhead and flashy aesthetics, I prioritize core fundamentals—semantic markup, performance, accessibility, and high-quality storytelling.

Whether I am engineering a responsive web page from the ground up or editing long-form video content, my approach relies on discipline and focus. If a user cannot navigate a site seamlessly or engage with a message effortlessly, the build isn't complete yet.
